## About REST AI

REST AI stands for Responsible, Ethical, Safe and Trustworthy AI.

It started from a straightforward observation: people trying to enter AI
governance, risk, compliance and security have very few places to start. The
regulations are recent, the standards sit behind paywalls, and most published
material assumes you already speak the language. Newcomers are left to piece
things together from fragments.

This site is an attempt to close some of that gap. It is independent, not
affiliated with any vendor or certification body, and the writing here reflects
practice rather than marketing.

The longer term aim is to build something in person as well: meetups and talks
in Tallinn and nearby cities, so people working on these problems around the
Baltics can find each other.

## About this site

Built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and served as static files. Subscriptions
are handled by a self-hosted [Listmonk](https://listmonk.app/) instance, so the
mailing list stays under my control and is not passed to a third party
processor. No tracking scripts, no advertising networks, no third party
analytics.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/beekayverma/restai_blog).
