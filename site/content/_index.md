## Responsible, Ethical, Safe and Trustworthy AI

REST AI is an initiative for awareness, education and support in AI governance,
risk, compliance and security.

AI GRCS is a young field, and it shows. The regulations are new, the standards
are behind paywalls, the guidance is written for people who already know the
vocabulary, and most of what is published online is either vendor marketing or
legal commentary written for lawyers. Someone starting out has very little to
stand on.

That gap is the reason this site exists.

## What you will find here

**Field notes, not thought leadership.** Write ups of how the EU AI Act, the
Cyber Resilience Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 27001 actually land when you sit
down to implement them, including the parts that are ambiguous or unresolved.

**A starting point for newcomers.** If you are moving into AI governance from
security, from compliance, from engineering or from policy, the first problem is
not difficulty. It is not knowing the shape of the map. Posts here try to give
you that shape before they give you detail.

**Honest uncertainty.** Where a requirement is genuinely unsettled, or where
practice has not caught up with the text, that gets said plainly rather than
smoothed over.

## Who it is for

People early in AI GRCS who need a way in. Engineers and founders who have
discovered that an AI system they built is now in regulatory scope. Security and
compliance practitioners extending into AI. Anyone in the Baltics and the wider
EU looking for others working on the same problems.

## Where this is going

The blog comes first. Beyond it, the intention is to build something local:
meetups and talks in Tallinn and nearby cities, so that people working on AI
governance in this region have somewhere to compare notes in person rather than
only reading about it.

If that sounds useful, subscribe below or [read the posts](/blog/).
